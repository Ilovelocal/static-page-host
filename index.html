Thoughtfully Designed

Built for
how you live

Every inch of The Bridgeford is purposefully arranged — all the space you need on a single level, with a layout that flows naturally from kitchen to living to outdoors.

Designed exclusively for Turtle Creek Homes · Town of Mosel, WI

The Bridgeford is available in two distinct layouts — see both floor plans and their room lists in the Floor Plans section ↓

01
Vaulted Ceilings

The open great room soars with vaulted ceilings, creating an airy, expansive feel from the moment you walk through the door.

02
Private Master Retreat

The primary bedroom sits apart from the secondary rooms, with an ensuite bath, walk-in closet, and its own private corridor.

03
Dedicated Home Office

A proper, separate office — not just a desk nook — keeps work life separated from home life without leaving the house.

04
Mudroom + Laundry

A generous mudroom with laundry keeps the mess contained. Drop zone, bench, and washer/dryer all in one organized space off the garage.

05
Open Kitchen + Dining

The kitchen island anchors the open living space, with sightlines straight through to the family room and a large pantry just steps away.

06
Covered Porch

A covered porch out front and back means you're outside no matter the weather — morning coffee, evening wind-down, all year round.
